# Real Estate Portfolio & Market Intelligence Analysis

An end-to-end Business Analyst and Data Analyst portfolio case study focused on real estate portfolio performance, project delays, apartment absorption, customer risk, promoter performance, and management reporting.

> This is an independent portfolio/practice case study based on publicly available real estate project registration data. It is not presented as a real client engagement.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Real estate portfolio data contains multiple dimensions including project status, completion dates, promoters, districts, apartment inventory, bookings, complaints, legal cases, project area, floors, FSI, and other development characteristics.

The objective of this project was to answer practical business questions that a real estate management or business intelligence team may receive from stakeholders.

The project follows an end-to-end Business Analyst workflow:

```text
Business Requirements
        ↓
Data Understanding
        ↓
Data Cleaning & Validation
        ↓
Feature Engineering
        ↓
KPI Development
        ↓

        ↓
Business Question Analysis
        ↓
Dashboard Development
        ↓
Executive Client Reporting

🎯 Business Objective

The analysis focuses on four major business areas:

Project Performance
Sales & Booking
Customer Risk
Project Characteristics

The objective was to transform raw project-level data into business-ready metrics, identify portfolio-level patterns, and create management-oriented reporting.
